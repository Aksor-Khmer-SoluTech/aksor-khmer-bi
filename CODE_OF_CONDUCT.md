# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
v2.1, in short:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Assume good faith; disagreements about technical direction are normal and
  welcome — keep them focused on the work.
- Maintainers may remove comments or contributors that violate this in
  repeated or severe cases.

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory or contacting a
maintainer directly rather than in a public issue.
