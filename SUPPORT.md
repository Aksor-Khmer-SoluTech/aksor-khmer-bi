# Support and funding

Aksor Khmer BI is **free and open source** (MIT). It is free to use, for anyone, for any purpose — no
paid edition, no feature locked away, no licence fee. The project is kept going by three things:
**donations**, **support subscriptions** and **collaboration**.

## Get help

### Community support (free)

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Aksor-Khmer-SoluTech/aksor-khmer-bi/issues).
  Include the version (portal footer), what you did, what you expected and what happened.
- **Ideas:** open an issue titled "Idea: …". Describe the problem first; the solution can follow.
- **Docs:** [`docs/`](docs) — also built into the portal (book icon, top bar).

Community support is best-effort: no guaranteed response time.

For people and organisations who rely on Aksor Khmer BI and want someone accountable. A subscription
buys **help**, not software — the software is the same for everyone.

Subscribe **per user** (an individual developer or a small team) or **per organisation** (everyone in
your organisation, one agreement):

## Support the project

### Donate

If Aksor Khmer BI saves you or your organisation time, a donation keeps it free and maintained. Donations
go to maintenance and security fixes, Khmer fonts and rendering tests, documentation, and the public
roadmap. Individuals and organisations are welcome; donors can be thanked in the README and release notes
if they'd like.

- `<GitHub Sponsors / Open Collective / bank transfer details>`

### Collaborate

We work with others who want Khmer-language document tooling to exist as a public good:

- **Organisations and agencies** — co-develop a feature you need, with the result released as open source
  for everyone, and share what you learn from running it.
- **Universities and researchers** — Khmer word segmentation, rendering and OCR are open problems.
- **Developers and translators** — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); Khmer documentation and
  real-world test documents are especially welcome.
- **Funders and development partners** — grants and programme funding for open digital public goods.

Write to [aksorkhmersolutech@gmail.com](mailto:aksorkhmersolutech@gmail.com) with what you have in mind.
